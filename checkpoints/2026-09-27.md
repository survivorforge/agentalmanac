## What these numbers do not say

- **The concentration figure is arithmetic; the classification is a judgement.** We label
  the dominant counterparty consumer commerce rather than agent-to-agent settlement, with
  the reasoning recorded and versioned by `wash_rule_version` (3.0). The headline holds
  whether or not you accept that label.
- **Raw volume is wash-inclusive.** Quote the validated or agent-to-agent figure.
- **Do not derive a "dead services" rate from listing counts.** Listings counts services we
  catalogue, not services we have tested. Only a few hundred have ever been HTTP-probed, so
  the rest are untested, not confirmed dead. We do not know the real rate and will not imply
  one until we measure it properly.
- **Coverage is partial and disclosed** at 79.44%, with the
  gaps listed on [/integrity](https://agentalmanac.org/integrity).
